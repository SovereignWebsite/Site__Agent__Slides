slides.d, account D: the deck template

How we make decks: one message a slide, the real thing on screen, one ask

Press the right arrow or swipe. P opens the presenter window, O the overview, F full screen.

This deck is the template and the method at once. Save the page to start your own.
Before a slide

Start from the reader: who they are, the one ask, and what they must believe

Who

Their role, what they know, how they will see it: a screen, a phone, a PDF.

Ask

One thing they should do after the last slide, with a date.

Belief

Three at most, each with evidence: a live page, a sourced number, a link.

The arc

Problem, then proof, then the ask

Static SVG only: script inside SVG rendered unreliably in past decks.

Every slide

The headline is a sentence; the picture carries it; the source is on the slide

  • No more than four steps on a slide, revealed as you speak
  • Never scrolls: if it does not fit at 1600 by 900, it is two slides
  • Notes hold what you say; the slide holds what they see
The ask

Send your deck to slides.d before you send it to anyone else
